# Shredder

![When paperwork has outlived its usefulness.](item:opencomputers:shredder)

The Shredder converts paper documents into [Paper Shreds](../item/papershreds.md).

It accepts paper, vanilla books, and [Printed Pages](../item/printedpage.md). Insert a supported item and the shredder processes it into recyclable shreds.

Paper Shreds can be crafted back into paper, making the shredder useful for disposing of old printouts without wasting all of the material.
